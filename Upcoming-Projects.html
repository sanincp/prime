Kerala Expansion & Public-Private Initiatives

Solid Waste Segregation & Resource Recovery Unit (Kerala)

In Collaboration with Haritha Keralam Mission (Green Kerala Mission)

Prime Plastic Recovery is expanding its circular economy footprint into Kerala through a dedicated Solid Waste Segregation and Resource Recovery Unit, established in a suitable location in accordance with Kerala State Pollution Control Board guidelines and relevant Government approvals, aligned with the Haritha Keralam Mission's zero-waste and litter-free state initiatives.

Project Overview

Designed to strengthen decentralized waste management across Local Self-Government Institutions (LSGIs), this upcoming facility bridges the gap between local door-to-door collection networks—such as the Haritha Karma Sena—and high-grade industrial recycling.

Key Focus Areas & Planned Capabilities

  • Multi-Stream Dry Waste Segregation: High-efficiency sorting lines to separate post-consumer plastics (HDPE, LDPE, PP, PET, MLP) from other non-biodegradable dry waste collected from households, commercial hubs, and public institutions.

  • Material Collection & Resource Recovery Integration: Seamless forward linkage with ward-level Material Collection Facilities (MCFs) and cluster-level Resource Recovery Facilities (RRFs) to prevent landfill accumulation and open burning.

  • Pre-Processing & Baling Infrastructure: Automated baling, shredding, and washing systems to convert segregated plastic streams into clean, transport-ready bales and high-purity regrind for manufacturing and polymer-modified road construction.

  • Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) Compliance: Establishing traceable, transparent recycling workflows that enable brands and local bodies to meet state and national EPR targets.

  • Community & Women-Led Workforce Empowerment: Working alongside local green task forces to provide safer working conditions, technical training, and sustainable value-chain support.

Additional Upcoming Kerala Pipeline Initiatives
