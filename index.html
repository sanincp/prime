Who We Are

About Us & Our Mission

Prime Plastic Recovery is dedicated to closing the loop on industrial and commercial plastic waste. We work with businesses to recover post-industrial and post-consumer plastics that might otherwise be discarded, treating each material stream as a resource with the potential for a useful second life. Through careful sourcing, material identification, and quality checks, we prepare plastics for responsible processing and help keep valuable materials in circulation.

Combining advanced sorting technology, dependable logistics, and ethical processing standards, we transform recovered plastics into high-grade reusable raw materials for manufacturers. From collection and handling to sorting and delivery, we focus on consistency and reliable coordination at every stage. This approach helps businesses reduce their environmental footprint, lower raw material costs, and access a dependable supply of recycled materials—while contributing to a more circular and resource-efficient plastics industry.
