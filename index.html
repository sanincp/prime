Who We Are

About Us & Our Mission

At Prime Plastic Recovery LLP, we don’t look at plastic as scrap—we treat it as a high-value industrial resource. While our corporate presence expands into South India with an exclusive focus on the Kerala sourcing market, our operational foundation is built on deep-rooted, multi-generational recycling expertise.

We bridge the gap between high-volume raw plastic aggregation in the South and India's massive manufacturing hubs. By implementing rigorous on-ground quality control checkpoints, professional logistics management, and absolute pollution board compliance, we ensure that every load delivered is prime grade.
