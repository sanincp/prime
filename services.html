Services

What We Offer

Service 01

Industrial Plastic Scrap Purchasing

We buy post-industrial plastic scrap directly from manufacturers, molding facilities, and distribution centers. We offer competitive market rates, transparent grading, and prompt payment for runners, purgings, off-spec parts, and production trimmings.

Service 02

Commercial Collection & Logistics

Eliminate storage bottlenecks with scheduled pickups and container drop-offs. We provide roll-off bins, gaylord boxes, and balers, then coordinate dependable transportation for on-time facility clearance.

Connect Now
Service 03

Sorting, Granulation & Toll Processing

We handle complex plastic streams through precision sorting, shredding, washing, and granulation. Closed-loop toll processing converts your scrap into clean, production-ready regrind for internal reuse.

Service 04

Custom Resin & Regrind Supply

We supply quality-tested recycled HDPE, LDPE, PP, PET, PVC, and ABS tailored to your manufacturing specifications. Every batch is checked for melt flow, color consistency, and structural integrity.

Service 05

Zero-Landfill Waste Audits & Consulting

Our sustainability specialists assess your facility’s waste output to identify recovery opportunities. We design compliant, cost-effective recycling workflows and provide diversion documentation to support ESG reporting and environmental compliance.
